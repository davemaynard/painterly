painterly

adjective. Of a painting or its style: characterised by visible brushwork and the rendering of form through colour and tone rather than by line.

Pick a photo and watch it get painted: a big brush lays in the underpainting, finer brushes return only where the picture still disagrees with the photo, and every stroke runs along the image instead of across it. Same photo, same seed, same painting.
