Fox at the edge of the wood

Every mark is a simulated tool moving simulated paint. Drops go straight onto the canvas; a wide brush spreads them, a knife pats in the dark, a steel-wool scrubber pounces it all while wet. Paint mixes as pigment does, stands up off the canvas, and sets when the hair dryer comes out.
